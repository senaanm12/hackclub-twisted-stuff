1. Heading Elements and Paragraphs

  1. H1 (h1) Element: The main heading used for the primary Title or Subject for a page.
  2. H2 (h2) Element: Major Subheading used to separate the primary sections of your page.
  3. H3 (h3) Element: Sub-Subheadings. Used for subsections under h2.
  4. H4 (h4) Element: Detailed Subsections. Used for deeper nested content under an h3.
  5. H5 (h5) Element: Minor Points. Rarely used, but is available for categorization under h4.
  6. H6 (h6) Element: The least significant heading level for nested items.
  7. Paragraph (p) Element: Used to hold the actual body text and content of the page.

2. Structural Containers (Semantic Layout)

  1. Navigation (nav) Element: Navigation Links. Wraps your main menu links or table of contents.
  2. Main (main) Element: The primary content area of the page.

Thematic Grouping

  1. Aside (aside) Element: Sidebar Content. Holds secondary info, like related links or author bios.
  2. Footer (footer) Element: Page bottom. Contains copyright notices, privacy links and contact info.

3. Text Formatting & Inline Elements

  1. HyperLink (a) Element: Connects your page to another website or file via the href attribute.
  2. Important text (strong) Element: Boldens text to show urgent or important words.
  3. Emphasized text (em) Element: Italicizes words to indicate verbal emphasis or stress.
  4. Line Break (br) Element: Forces text to jump to the next line.

4. Organized Lists

  1. Unordered List (ul) Element: Creates a bulleted list of items.
  2. Ordered List (ol) Element: Creates a numbered list of items.
  3. List Item (li) Element: Represents an individual item within (ol) or (ul).
